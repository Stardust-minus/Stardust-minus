## About Me

Undergrad (class of 2028) passionate about **Cloud Native** infrastructure and **High Performance Computing**. Currently working at **39 AI / FishAudio** as an Infrastructure Engineer, specializing in GPU cluster operations at scale.

Previously an **Audio Algorithm Engineer** focusing on TTS/SVC model development. Transitioned to Infrastructure to build the systems that power AI — from bare-metal GPU clusters to Kubernetes-orchestrated training pipelines.

---

## What I Do

- **Compute** — Operate **100–1,000+ GPU** training clusters on bare metal and cloud, scheduled with **Slurm** (training) and **Kubernetes** (services & MLOps).
- **Storage** — Run **petabyte-scale Ceph** (object storage + CephFS) that feeds our training data pipelines.
- **Network** — Run our own ASN [**AS402567**](https://bgp.he.net/AS402567) with a BGP edge and **100Gbps+** public uplinks.
- **Automation** — Bare-metal provisioning through day-2 operations with **Ansible**; observability with **Prometheus** and **Grafana**.

All of it is the substrate beneath FishAudio's production TTS models.
